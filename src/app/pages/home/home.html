Our Academic & Research Initiatives

VEI Technologies Private Limited is an Indian IT and software services company committed to bridging the gap between academic learning and industry-ready expertise. Since its incorporation, VEI has grown beyond conventional software development into a dedicated platform for education, applied research, and doctoral-level academic support — serving students, faculty, and research scholars across engineering, computer science, and emerging technology domains.

PhD & Research Scholar Support

VEI's research mentorship pairs scholars with experienced technical consultants across computer science, data science, AI, machine learning, and cybersecurity. Support spans the full research lifecycle — problem identification, literature review, methodology design, implementation, and publication — including hands-on help with data analysis, algorithm design, simulation, and modern research tooling.

Faculty Development Programs

VEI conducts FDPs, workshops, and short-term training modules to help educators stay current with fast-evolving technology — covering AI applications, data science, cloud computing, and software engineering best practices, so faculty bring industry-relevant knowledge back into their classrooms.

Academic Collaboration

VEI partners with institutions on joint research initiatives, technical paper development, and publication support, upholding ethical research practices and academic integrity throughout every engagement.
